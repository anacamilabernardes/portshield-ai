# Independent research and engineering prototype

PortShield AI is independently authored portfolio software using public and synthetic material.
It is not an official MPOR/PIDP system, approved policy, production deployment, or a statement of
institutional sponsorship. Architectural alignment with principles supplied in a PIDP proposal
does not imply institutional adoption. No nonpublic MPOR information is included or needed.

Every included port ID, operational event and demonstration scenario is fictional. Outputs do
not establish actual infrastructure risk, legal compliance, safe navigation, or incident status.
The project never executes operational actions; all outputs require qualified human review.
Only English templates are evaluated; Portuguese capability remains unvalidated.

LoRA/QLoRA, quantization and vLLM workflows are implemented as research pipelines; no trained
checkpoint or GPU benchmark result is bundled. Null means unmeasured, never zero resource use.
Toy tensor-network experiments use standard SVD and Tensor Train/MPO ideas on a 16x16 matrix.
They do not implement, reproduce, reverse-engineer, or claim equivalence to proprietary CompactifAI.
No performance claim or endorsement by Multiverse Computing is made.

MIT covers repository-authored code, synthetic data and original source summaries. External
weights, documents, dependencies and container images retain their respective licenses.
